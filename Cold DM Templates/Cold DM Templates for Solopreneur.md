# Cold DM Templates for Solopreneurs

Use these templates to reach out to potential clients via LinkedIn, Twitter, Email, Instagram, or WhatsApp.

---

## 1. Simple Cold DM

Hi [Name],

I came across your [website/page/business] and noticed that you are [something specific].

I help [type of business] with [service] to get [result].

I recorded a quick idea for you:
[Your idea]

Would you like me to send it?

– [Your Name]

---

## 2. Problem-Based DM

Hi [Name],

I noticed that many [their industry] businesses struggle with [problem].

I help businesses solve this by [your solution].

If you're interested, I can share a quick idea for [their business name].

No pressure just thought it might help.

– [Your Name]

---

## 3. Value First DM

Hi [Name],

I made a quick suggestion for your [website/social media/ads].

Here it is:
[1–2 helpful tips]

If you want, I can help you implement this.

– [Your Name]

---

## 4. Portfolio DM

Hi [Name],

I recently helped [similar business] get [result].

I would love to help [their business name] do something similar.

Can I send you a quick idea?

– [Your Name]

---

## 5. Short DM (Very Effective)

Hi [Name],

Quick question, are you looking for help with [result]?

– [Your Name]

---

## 6. Follow-Up DM

Hi [Name],

Just wanted to follow up in case you missed my last message.

I had an idea for [their business name] that could help with [result].

Let me know if you want me to send it.

– [Your Name]

---

## 7. Email Version

Subject: Quick idea for [Business Name]

Hi [Name],

I was looking at [their website/business] and I noticed [something].

I help [type of business] get [result] by [what you do].

I have a quick idea for you:
[idea]

If you're interested, I can explain it in 10 minutes.

Best,  
[Your Name]

---

## Tips for Cold Outreach

- Personalize the first line
- Keep messages short
- Focus on results, not services
- Send 20 messages per day
- Always follow up
- Track replies
- Improve your script over time
